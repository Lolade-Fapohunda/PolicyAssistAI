# Petadel Technology Services
## Information Security Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes security requirements for protecting Petadel Technology Services (PTS) information, systems, devices, and data from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or destruction.

## 2. Information Protection

Employees must protect PTS confidential and sensitive information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Employees must only access information necessary to perform their assigned responsibilities.

Employees must not intentionally disclose confidential PTS information to unauthorized individuals.

## 3. Account and Password Security

Employees are responsible for protecting their PTS account credentials.

Passwords and authentication credentials must not be shared with another person.

Employees must use PTS-approved authentication methods when accessing company systems.

If an employee believes their credentials have been compromised, they must report the incident to Information Security immediately.

## 4. Access Control

Access to PTS systems and information must be based on business need and the user's authorized level of access.

Employees must not attempt to bypass access controls or obtain access to information they are not authorized to access.

Access privileges may be reviewed and modified when an employee changes roles or responsibilities.

## 5. Company Devices

Employees must use PTS-approved devices when required to access company systems or sensitive information.

Employees are responsible for protecting company-issued devices from loss, theft, unauthorized use, and damage.

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to Information Security as soon as possible.

## 6. Remote Access

Employees accessing PTS systems remotely must use approved security controls and authentication methods.

Employees must take reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized individuals from viewing or accessing PTS information while working remotely.

Confidential PTS information must not be stored on unauthorized personal devices or unauthorized cloud storage services.

## 7. Data Storage and Transmission

Employees must store PTS information only in approved systems and locations.

Sensitive information must be transmitted using PTS-approved methods.

Employees must not transfer confidential PTS information to unauthorized external services.

## 8. Security Incidents

Employees must promptly report suspected security incidents to Information Security.

Examples of incidents that should be reported include:

- Suspected unauthorized account access
- Lost or stolen company devices
- Accidental disclosure of confidential information
- Suspected malware or phishing activity
- Unauthorized access to PTS systems

Employees should not attempt to conceal a suspected security incident.

## 9. Phishing and Suspicious Communications

Employees must exercise caution when opening unexpected attachments, clicking links, or providing credentials in response to electronic communications.

Suspicious communications should be reported through the security reporting process established by PTS.

## 10. Acceptable Use

PTS systems and technology resources must primarily be used for legitimate business purposes.

Employees must not use PTS systems to conduct unauthorized activities or attempt to compromise the security of company systems.

## 11. Third-Party Access

Third parties may access PTS systems or information only when such access has been authorized.

Employees must not provide unauthorized individuals or organizations with access to PTS systems or confidential information.

## 12. Policy Violations

Failure to comply with this policy may result in removal of system access, disciplinary action, or other appropriate action.

Security violations may also be referred to appropriate authorities when required.

## 13. Questions

Questions regarding information security requirements should be directed to Information Security.

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the organization's employee leave benefits and requirements.

## 2. Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive **20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year**.

Employees may request PTO through the organization's approved leave management process.

## 3. Parental Leave

Eligible employees may receive **up to 16 weeks of parental leave** following the birth or adoption of a child.

Eligibility is determined according to applicable organizational requirements.

## 4. Sick Leave

Full-time employees receive **10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year**.

## 5. Leave Approval

Employees should submit leave requests through the approved leave management process.

Managers are responsible for reviewing requests according to applicable organizational policy.

## 6. Policy Authority

This document is the **active and approved version** of the Employee Leave Policy.

Previous versions of this policy are superseded and should not be used as current guidance.

## 7. Questions and Exceptions

Questions regarding eligibility or requests that fall outside the standard policy should be directed to Human Resources.

Human Resources may determine whether additional review or an exception process is required.

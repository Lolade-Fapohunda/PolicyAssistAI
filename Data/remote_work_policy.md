# Petadel Technology Services
## Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the requirements and expectations for employees who are approved to work remotely.

## 2. Eligibility

Remote work is available to eligible employees whose job responsibilities can be performed effectively outside a Petadel Technology Services (PTS) office.

Remote work eligibility is determined by the employee's manager in consultation with Human Resources.

Remote work is not an automatic employee benefit and may be modified or discontinued based on business needs, performance, security requirements, or operational considerations.

## 3. Remote Work Schedule

Approved employees may work remotely up to **three days per week**.

Employees are expected to work from the PTS office on the remaining scheduled workdays unless their manager has approved a different arrangement.

The employee's manager may establish specific in-office days based on team and business requirements.

## 4. Work Hours and Availability

Remote employees must maintain their regularly scheduled working hours unless an alternative schedule has been approved by their manager.

Employees must remain available during scheduled working hours through approved PTS communication and collaboration systems.

Remote work does not change an employee's expected workload, responsibilities, or performance standards.

## 5. Work Location

Employees must work from a safe and appropriate location that allows them to perform their responsibilities effectively.

Employees must protect confidential PTS information from unauthorized access or disclosure when working remotely.

Working from a public location is permitted only when reasonable safeguards are in place to prevent unauthorized access to company information.

## 6. Technology and Equipment

Employees must use PTS-approved devices, applications, and systems when accessing company information remotely.

Employees are responsible for protecting company equipment assigned to them.

Employees must promptly report lost or stolen company equipment to their manager and the Information Security team.

## 7. Security Requirements

Employees working remotely must follow all PTS information security requirements.

Employees must not share PTS passwords or authentication credentials with another person.

Confidential company information must not be stored on unauthorized personal devices or unauthorized cloud storage services.

Employees must use approved authentication and security controls when accessing PTS systems remotely.

## 8. Meetings and Collaboration

Remote employees are expected to participate in required meetings and maintain reasonable communication with their teams.

Employees must have access to the communication tools necessary to participate in scheduled meetings and perform their assigned work.

## 9. Manager Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for determining whether remote work arrangements support business and team requirements.

Managers may adjust an employee's remote work schedule when operational needs require additional in-office attendance.

Managers should communicate schedule changes to employees in advance whenever reasonably possible.

## 10. Policy Violations

Failure to comply with this policy may result in modification or removal of remote work privileges and may result in disciplinary action when appropriate.

## 11. Questions

Questions regarding remote work eligibility or scheduling should be directed to the employee's manager or Human Resources.

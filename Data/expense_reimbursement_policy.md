# Petadel Technology Services
## Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for submitting and receiving reimbursement for business-related expenses incurred on behalf of Petadel Technology Services (PTS).

## 2. Eligible Expenses

PTS may reimburse employees for reasonable and necessary business expenses incurred while performing authorized company business.

Examples of potentially reimbursable expenses include:

- Business travel
- Lodging
- Ground transportation
- Business meals
- Approved supplies
- Registration fees for approved conferences or professional events

Expenses must be directly related to legitimate business activities.

## 3. Prior Approval

Employees must obtain manager approval before incurring any significant or unusual business expense.

Business travel must be approved before the travel is booked.

Employees should not assume that an expense will be reimbursed simply because it was incurred for a business purpose.

## 4. Receipts and Documentation

Employees must provide an itemized receipt or other acceptable documentation for reimbursable expenses.

Expense submissions must include:

- Date of the expense
- Amount
- Business purpose
- Vendor or merchant
- Applicable project, department, or cost center when required

Missing documentation may delay reimbursement or result in the expense being denied.

## 5. Expense Submission Deadline

Employees must submit reimbursement requests within **30 calendar days** after the expense is incurred.

Late submissions may require additional approval from the employee's manager and Finance.

## 6. Business Meals

Business meals may be reimbursed when they have a legitimate business purpose.

The expense submission must identify the business purpose and the individuals participating in the meal when required by Finance.

Personal meals that are not authorized business expenses are not reimbursable.

## 7. Travel Expenses

Employees traveling on approved PTS business may be reimbursed for reasonable transportation and lodging expenses.

Employees are expected to select reasonably priced travel options consistent with business needs.

Personal upgrades, entertainment, and other expenses unrelated to authorized business activities are not reimbursable.

## 8. Personal Expenses

PTS does not reimburse personal expenses.

Examples include personal entertainment, personal purchases, traffic or parking violations, and expenses incurred for the personal benefit of an employee unless specifically authorized under another PTS policy.

## 9. Corporate Payment Methods

When a PTS corporate card or other company-approved payment method is available and required for a particular expense, employees should use the approved payment method.

Employees must not use company payment methods for unauthorized personal purchases.

## 10. Approval and Reimbursement

Expense reports are reviewed by the appropriate manager and Finance before reimbursement.

Finance may request additional documentation or clarification before approving a reimbursement request.

Approved reimbursements are processed through the company's established payment process.

## 11. False or Misleading Claims

Employees must submit accurate expense information.

Submitting false receipts, intentionally misrepresenting expenses, or requesting reimbursement for personal expenses may result in disciplinary action.

## 12. Policy Violations

Failure to comply with this policy may result in denial of reimbursement and may result in disciplinary action when appropriate.

## 13. Questions

Questions regarding expense eligibility or reimbursement procedures should be directed to Finance or the employee's manager.

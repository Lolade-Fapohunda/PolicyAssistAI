# Petadel Technology Services
## Attendance Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes attendance and punctuality expectations for employees of Petadel Technology Services (PTS).

## 2. Attendance Expectations

Employees are expected to report to work as scheduled and be ready to perform their assigned responsibilities at the beginning of their scheduled work hours.

Employees are responsible for maintaining reliable attendance and communicating promptly when they are unable to report to work as scheduled.

## 3. Standard Work Schedule

The standard full-time work schedule is **40 hours per week**, unless an employee's employment agreement or approved work arrangement specifies otherwise.

Employees must follow the work schedule established by their manager.

## 4. Tardiness

Employees who expect to arrive late must notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Repeated or unexplained tardiness may result in corrective action.

Employees are expected to accurately record their working time when timekeeping is required.

## 5. Absences

Employees who are unable to report to work must notify their manager before the start of their scheduled workday whenever reasonably possible.

Employees should provide an expected return-to-work date when known.

Unreported or unauthorized absences may be treated as attendance violations.

## 6. Planned Time Away

Planned absences should be requested and approved in accordance with applicable PTS leave policies and the employee's department procedures.

Employees should provide reasonable advance notice for planned absences whenever possible.

Approval of planned time away is subject to applicable policy requirements and business needs.

## 7. Emergency Absences

PTS recognizes that emergencies may prevent an employee from providing advance notice.

In an emergency, employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Managers should work with Human Resources when an absence may involve protected leave or another applicable workplace accommodation.

## 8. Remote Employees

Employees approved for remote work are subject to the same attendance and availability expectations as employees working from a PTS office.

Remote work does not eliminate the requirement to work scheduled hours or notify a manager of an absence.

## 9. Medical and Protected Leave

Employees who may qualify for protected leave should contact Human Resources.

Attendance-related decisions must be administered consistently with applicable PTS leave policies and applicable law.

Managers should consult Human Resources before taking corrective action when an absence may involve protected leave.

## 10. Attendance Records

PTS may maintain attendance and timekeeping records to support payroll, workforce planning, policy administration, and performance management.

Employees are responsible for providing accurate information when required to record working time or attendance.

## 11. Policy Violations

Failure to comply with attendance requirements, including repeated unapproved absences, excessive unexcused tardiness, or failure to follow notification procedures, may result in corrective or disciplinary action.

## 12. Questions

Questions regarding attendance requirements should be directed to the employee's manager or Human Resources.
